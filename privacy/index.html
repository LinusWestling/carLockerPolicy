Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This policy explains how carLocker handles your information. The short version: your car data stays on your iPhone, and the developer of carLocker, Linus Westling, never sees it. The only information that leaves your iPhone on its own is subscription information: a random customer ID and your purchase details, shared with Apple and RevenueCat to manage Pro.

What the app stores

carLocker is a place to keep everything about your car. Depending on what you choose to add, it can hold cars, photos, documents such as registration, ownership and insurance papers, insurance policy details, service and issue history, mileage, purchase price and value estimates, tire information, and identifiers such as the VIN and licence plate.

Where your data lives

All of this data is stored only on your device, inside the app's sandbox. There is no account and there is no server. The app's own code makes no network requests and never sends your car data anywhere. The developer of carLocker cannot see, access or recover your data.

What the developer collects

Nothing about your cars or you. The developer of carLocker does not receive your cars, photos, documents, values, mileage or insurance details. Specifically:

  • no analytics
  • no ads
  • no tracking
  • no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking (the only third-party SDK that connects to the internet is RevenueCat, for subscriptions, described below)
  • no crash reporting service
  • no account, email address, name or phone number is asked for or stored

If you have opted in to share diagnostics with app developers in iOS settings, Apple controls that data and its handling. The developer of carLocker does not run a service for it.

Device permissions

Each permission is optional and asked for only when you use the matching feature. You can change them at any time in iOS Settings.

  • Camera: used only to take photos of your car and to scan documents such as registration, insurance papers and receipts. Scanning is done on your device with Apple's document scanner. Images stay on your device.
  • Photo library and Files: used only to add photos or documents that you pick yourself, and to open a backup file you choose to restore. The app does not browse your library or your files on its own.
  • Face ID or device passcode: optional, used to lock and unlock the Vault and to protect backup and export. iOS (LocalAuthentication) handles this. The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Local notifications: optional, used for reminders such as service, tire switches, inspection, insurance renewal and an occasional nudge to update your mileage. They are scheduled on your device and no server is involved.

Backups and exports

You can create a backup file, export your data as CSV files, or create a PDF history of a car. These files are created on your iPhone and go nowhere unless you send them. They leave your device only when you share or save them yourself with the iOS share sheet or the Files app, for example to iCloud Drive, AirDrop or Mail.

A backup can optionally be protected with a password. A password-protected backup is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) before it is shared; the password is never stored or sent anywhere, so if you forget it the backup cannot be opened by you or by the developer. A backup without a password is not encrypted, so anyone who gets the file can read it. Keep backup files somewhere safe. Once you share a file, that copy is your responsibility and is governed by the privacy practices of the destination service. Restoring a backup reads the file you choose and replaces the data in the app.

Separately, your iPhone's own iCloud or computer (iTunes/Finder) backup may include carLocker's data, as it does for most apps. That backup is made by iOS and is controlled by Apple, not by the app or the developer.

Subscriptions and purchases

carLocker offers an optional auto-renewable subscription, carLocker Pro, billed monthly or yearly, with a one-week free trial for eligible new subscribers. Prices are shown in the app. Apple processes all payments, and the developer never receives your payment details. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (privacy policy). RevenueCat receives:

  • a random customer ID, created on your device the first time it is needed and stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only (it is not created from your name, email, Apple Account or any other personal detail, and it is not shared between your devices). It stays on the device after you delete the app so that a reinstall is recognised as the same customer
  • purchase and receipt information from the App Store (product, price, currency, dates, trial and renewal status)
  • basic device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, locale/country, and IP address as part of normal network requests)

RevenueCat links your purchase information to that random customer ID, so Apple's privacy label for carLocker lists Purchase History and a User ID as collected and linked to the ID. The ID cannot be tied to you as a person by carLocker, because carLocker has no account and holds no name or email. This information is used only to unlock Pro, to restore purchases, and for aggregate subscription analytics, for example trial conversion rates. It is not used for advertising, there is no tracking across apps, and the app does not use the IDFA or any advertising identifier. Your car data is never sent to RevenueCat.

Children

carLocker is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete your car data at any time in the app (Settings, Erase all data) or by deleting the app. Because the developer holds no copy, this removes it completely from the app. Copies you exported or shared elsewhere, and any copy in your own iPhone backup, must be deleted separately. The random customer ID stays in your device's Keychain after you delete the app (so your Pro subscription is recognised if you reinstall). It contains no personal information. If you want the purchase record linked to it deleted, email the contact address below and the developer will pass the request on to RevenueCat.
