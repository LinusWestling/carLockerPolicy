Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This policy explains how carLocker handles your information. The short version: your car data stays on your device (iPhone or Android phone), and the developer of carLocker, Linus Westling, never sees it. The only information that leaves your device on its own is subscription information: a random customer ID and your purchase details, shared with the app store (Apple App Store or Google Play) and RevenueCat to manage Pro.

What the app stores

carLocker is a place to keep everything about your car. Depending on what you choose to add, it can hold cars, photos, documents such as registration, ownership and insurance papers, insurance policy details, service and issue history, mileage, purchase price and value estimates, tire information, and identifiers such as the VIN and licence plate.

Where your data lives

All of this data is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage (the app sandbox on iPhone, the app-private storage on Android). There is no account and there is no server. The app's own code makes no network requests and never sends your car data anywhere. The developer of carLocker cannot see, access or recover your data.

What the developer collects

Nothing about your cars or you. The developer of carLocker does not receive your cars, photos, documents, values, mileage or insurance details. Specifically:

  • no analytics
  • no ads
  • no tracking
  • no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising or tracking (the only third-party SDK that connects to the internet is RevenueCat, for subscriptions, described below)
  • no crash reporting service
  • no account, email address, name or phone number is asked for or stored

If you have opted in to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings (iOS, or Android usage and diagnostics), Apple or Google controls that data and its handling. The developer of carLocker does not run a service for it.

Device permissions

Each permission is optional and asked for only when you use the matching feature. You can change them at any time in iOS Settings or Android system settings.

  • Camera: used only to take photos of your car and to scan documents such as registration, insurance papers and receipts. Scanning is done on your device. On iPhone it uses Apple's document scanner. On Android it uses the Google ML Kit Document Scanner, which is provided by Google Play services and processes the images on your device; the images stay on your device and are not sent to the developer. Google Play services is governed by Google's privacy policy.
  • Photo library and Files: used only to add photos or documents that you pick yourself, and to open a backup file you choose to restore. The app does not browse your library or your files on its own. On Android this uses the system photo picker and the system file picker, so the app only receives the items you select and needs no broad storage permission.
  • Face ID or device passcode (iPhone), fingerprint, face unlock or device screen lock (Android): optional, used to lock and unlock the Vault and to protect backup and export. The operating system handles this (LocalAuthentication on iOS, BiometricPrompt on Android). The app never receives your biometric data.
  • Local notifications: optional, used for reminders such as service, tire switches, inspection, insurance renewal and an occasional nudge to update your mileage. They are scheduled on your device and no server is involved. On Android the notification permission is requested on Android versions that require it, and the app does not use exact alarms, so a reminder may arrive a little after its scheduled time.

Backups and exports

You can create a backup file, export your data as CSV files, or create a PDF history of a car. These files are created on your device and go nowhere unless you send them. They leave your device only when you share or save them yourself, with the iOS share sheet or the Files app on iPhone, or with the Android share sheet or system file picker on Android, for example to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, AirDrop or Mail.

A backup can optionally be protected with a password. A password-protected backup is encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) before it is shared; the password is never stored or sent anywhere, so if you forget it the backup cannot be opened by you or by the developer. A backup without a password is not encrypted, so anyone who gets the file can read it. Keep backup files somewhere safe. Once you share a file, that copy is your responsibility and is governed by the privacy practices of the destination service. Restoring a backup reads the file you choose and replaces the data in the app.

Separately, your iPhone's own iCloud or computer (iTunes/Finder) backup may include carLocker's data, as it does for most apps. That backup is made by iOS and is controlled by Apple, not by the app or the developer. On Android, the system's own backup (Google Auto Backup to your Google Account, or a device-to-device transfer) may likewise include some of the app's data, such as the random customer ID described below. That backup is made by Android and is controlled by Google and your device settings, not by the app or the developer.

Subscriptions and purchases

carLocker offers an optional auto-renewable subscription, carLocker Pro, billed monthly or yearly, with a one-week free trial for eligible new subscribers. Prices are shown in the app. Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) processes all payments, depending on where you installed the app, and the developer never receives your payment details. To manage subscription status, the app uses RevenueCat, Inc. (privacy policy). RevenueCat receives:

  • a random customer ID, created on your device the first time it is needed (it is not created from your name, email, Apple Account, Google Account or any other personal detail, and it is not shared between your devices). On iPhone it is stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only and stays on the device after you delete the app, so that a reinstall is recognised as the same customer. On Android it is stored in app storage that is included in Android's Auto Backup, so that a reinstall on the same Google Account is recognised as the same customer; that backup copy is held by Google as part of your Android backup
  • purchase and receipt information from the App Store or Google Play (product, price, currency, dates, trial and renewal status)
  • basic device and app information (device model, OS version, app version, locale/country, and IP address as part of normal network requests)

RevenueCat links your purchase information to that random customer ID, so Apple's privacy label and Google Play's Data safety section for carLocker list Purchase History and a User ID as collected and linked to the ID, for app functionality and analytics. This data is not sold and not shared for advertising, and it is encrypted in transit. The ID cannot be tied to you as a person by carLocker, because carLocker has no account and holds no name or email. This information is used only to unlock Pro, to restore purchases, and for aggregate subscription analytics, for example trial conversion rates. It is not used for advertising, there is no tracking across apps, and the app does not use the IDFA, the Android advertising ID or any other advertising identifier. Your car data is never sent to RevenueCat.

Children

carLocker is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

You can delete your car data at any time in the app (Settings, Erase all data) or by deleting the app. Because the developer holds no copy, this removes it completely from the app. Copies you exported or shared elsewhere, and any copy in your own iPhone or Android backup, must be deleted separately. The random customer ID stays in your device's Keychain after you delete the app on iPhone, and on Android it can be restored from your Google backup if you reinstall on the same Google Account (so your Pro subscription is recognised if you reinstall). It contains no personal information. If you want the purchase record linked to it deleted, email the contact address below and the developer will pass the request on to RevenueCat.
